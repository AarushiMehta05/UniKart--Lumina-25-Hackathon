## 🎯 1. Introduction  
UniKart is a smart campus-centric delivery platform designed specifically for large college settings.  
It integrates **food delivery**, **grocery and daily-use item delivery**, and **print-shop doorstep service**, all tailored for hostellers and day scholars alike.  
Our goal: streamline campus life, save time, reduce queues, and boost student convenience.

---

## 🧠 2. Problem Statement  
- Students often waste valuable time standing in long queues at campus cafes, print shops or stores.  
- Hostellers might skip meals due to time constraints or lack of seating; day scholars struggle with overcrowded facilities.  
- Basic errands (groceries, printing) consume academic focus and disrupt schedules.  
- There’s a need for a unified, efficient solution to improve daily student experience on-campus.

---

## ✅ 3. Our Solution  
### How UniKart Works:  
1. **Food Delivery** – Order from the campus café and get meals delivered directly to hostel rooms or academic blocks.  
2. **Groceries & Daily-Use Items** – Access the campus store virtually and receive doorstep delivery of essentials.  
3. **Print-Shop Integration** – Upload documents online, select print options, and get copies delivered without waiting in line.  
### Key Features  
- Web/Mobile interface for ordering  
- Real time tracking  
- Secure digital payments (UPI, student wallet)  
- Student delivery partners within campus for fast fulfillment  
- Optimised for short-radius campus logistics  

---

## ✨ 4. Impact & Benefits  
**For Students:**  
- Saves hours spent on errands & queues  
- Improves access to meals & essentials  
- Greater focus on academics and campus life  
**For Vendors & Campus:**  
- Expanded reach for campus cafés, stores & print shops  
- Reduced crowding and better service flow  
- Data insights for student demand and inventory  
**Overall:**  
UniKart enhances campus efficiency, supports student well-being and fosters a modern “smart campus” experience.

---

## 🌱 5. Sustainability & Long-Term Viability  
- **Economic:** Low-cost model with small delivery fee, subscription plan and vendor partnerships ensures revenue.  
- **Social:** Creates micro-jobs for student delivery partners; promotes community engagement.  
- **Environmental:** Use of eco-friendly packaging, short delivery radius reduces emissions; digital transactions reduce paper waste.  
- **Scalable:** Built on cloud infrastructure; easily replicable to other campuses with minimal setup.

- **Database:** Firestore / MySQL  
- **Payment Integration:** UPI / Student Wallet  
- **Hosting:** Vercel / Netlify (frontend), Firebase Functions / AWS Lambda (backend)
